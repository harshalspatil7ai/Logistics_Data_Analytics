# Logistics Delivery Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes logistics delivery performance to identify the key factors affecting delivery delays, delivery time, cost, and overall operational efficiency.

The analysis uses Python and data analysis libraries to explore delivery patterns, identify operational bottlenecks, and generate actionable business insights for improving logistics performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall delivery performance
* Identify major factors contributing to delivery delays
* Compare delivery performance across different weather conditions
* Analyze delivery time and expected delivery time
* Study the impact of distance, package weight, vehicle type, and delivery mode
* Evaluate delivery costs and customer ratings
* Generate actionable insights for logistics optimization

## 📊 Key Insights

The analysis investigates how different operational and environmental factors influence delivery performance.

Key areas of focus include:

* Delay rate across weather conditions
* Relationship between delivery distance and delivery time
* Differences in performance across vehicle types
* Delivery-mode performance
* Impact of package characteristics on delivery time
* Cost and rating patterns
* Identification of potential operational bottlenecks

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, logistics operations can be improved by:

* Monitoring high-delay weather conditions and planning accordingly
* Optimizing delivery routes based on distance and expected delivery time
* Allocating suitable vehicles according to delivery requirements
* Improving resource allocation during high-risk delivery conditions
* Using historical delivery data for better planning and forecasting
* Continuously monitoring KPIs such as delay rate, delivery time, cost, and customer rating
